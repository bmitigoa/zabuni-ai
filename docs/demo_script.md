# Demo video script: 3 minutes, one unedited take

**Setup before recording (not part of the 3 minutes).** Private browser window at 125 % zoom on https://zabuni-ai.streamlit.app, already awake (open it 5 minutes earlier and wait for "Data ready"); sidebar visible; GROQ quota checked (`logs/llm_usage.jsonl`, or run once the day before: a run is about 4,000 tokens); a second tab with `docs/sample_run/` is **not** needed. Do not type a Groq key or token anywhere on screen. Say numbers as they appear; do not read this script word for word.

| Time | On screen | Say (about) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00-0:15** | The app home, banner visible | "This is Zabuni AI: decision support for procurement units and internal audit. It reads Kenya's published tender awards, finds value-for-money and integrity leads, and every lead cites the public record behind it. It never recommends or awards a tender: the banner says so on every page." |
| **0:15-0:35** | Click the first example (Makueni County, 2022-2023) so the form fills; point at buyer, years; click **Run investigation** | "I name a buyer and years. A request with no buyer, supplier or period would not run at all: I'll show that at the end." |
| **0:35-1:05** | Live progress list: the **plan**, then tool calls scrolling (name, arguments, milliseconds) | "The agent plans its own checks from the tools it discovers on the MCP server: a search, splitting, price benchmark, price anomaly. Every call is shown with its arguments and duration, and logged." |
| **1:05-1:25** | Point at **follow-up** lines (price checks after the search; splitting after a price outlier), a **recovery** or fallback line if one appears | "Results change what it does next: a price outlier triggers a splitting and concentration check for that buyer. If a search finds too few comparables it widens it; if the model hits its quota it falls back to a second open-weights model and says so in the summary." |
| **1:25-1:55** | Scroll to the **risk board**: a merged card. Point at "Flagged award" (buyer, supplier, date, KES amount), then "Signals" (two multiples), then "Compared against: N awards (buyers, years)" with "wider than your request", the data-entry hint, the "provisional" badge; click an `ocid` link to show the stored record | "One card per award. The flagged award is shown apart from the awards it was compared with, and the card says why two checks give different multiples: they compare it with different sets. The amount looks like a possible data-entry error, so it says to verify with the source document. Thresholds are labelled provisional because no practitioner has validated them yet. Every citation links to the stored OCDS record." |
| **1:55-2:30** | **Review panel**: first flag **Approve** with a reason; second flag **Clarify** with a question, submit, show the agent's answer appearing on the card; third flag **Reject** with a reason; role selector visible | "Nothing enters the memo without a human. I approve one with a reason, ask the agent a question about another and it re-investigates that flag, and I reject one. Reasons are mandatory and I am recorded by role, not by name. A rejected flag is remembered and will not be raised again." |
| **2:30-2:45** | The **memo**: approved flags only, cited records, reasons, limitations, the "does not recommend or award any tender" statement; click **Download PDF** | "The memo lists approved flags only, with the reviewers' reasons and the data limitations." |
| **2:45-2:55** | New run: clear the form, type `hello`, **Run investigation** (the app says a scope is needed). *If you want to show the agent's own guard, use the CLI line instead: `python -m agent.run hello` -> "SCOPE NEEDED".* | "Failure mode: my first version turned the word 'hello' into forty flags about institutions nobody asked about. Now it asks for a buyer, a supplier or years and runs nothing." |
| **2:55-3:00** | The metric row under the memo (tokens, tool calls, estimated cost, model(s) used) | "This run used about four thousand tokens and well under a cent on the free tier. Code, evals and architecture are in the repository." |

**If something goes wrong on camera (unedited take, so decide in advance).** (1) Quota message: say "this is the quota guard working; the free tier is shared", then run the same query with `LLM_PROVIDER=ollama` locally off-camera and keep the take. (2) A slow synthesis: keep talking over the progress list about the guards (citation guard, scope check). (3) A 429 fallback line appears: point at it; it is a feature. Retake rather than editing.

**Checklist while recording.** Total under 3:00 (target 2:50). Show: plan, tool calls, a follow-up, a merged card with cited evidence, approve / clarify / reject with reasons, the memo, the scope guard on "hello", the cost line. Upload unlisted to YouTube and paste the link in the README banner and the submission form.
